# Code of Conduct

Runvora aims to be a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, demeaning, or hostile comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all Runvora repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers by contacting an organization owner through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, or restrict or ban participants who violate this code. A dedicated reporting channel will be added once one is established.

This code of conduct is a short, original summary of commonly accepted community standards.
